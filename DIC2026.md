## INTRODUCTION

New York University Shanghai Digital Innovation Challenge (NYUSHDIC) is a tech-based innovation competition hosted by the student organization NYUSH Digital Innovation Community. Students from NYUSH and other universities are welcome to form cross-disciplinary teams, use cutting-edge digital technology, and pitch innovative ideas that address real-world problems.
